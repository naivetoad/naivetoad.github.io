I'm a second-year master's student in the Computational Social Science program at the University of Chicago. My current research focuses on investigating representations of gender in role-playing video game dialogues using natural language processing techniques. 

I attended high school at [North Broward Preparatory School](https://www.nordangliaeducation.com/nbps-florida) (Coconut Creek, FL), and earned double bachelor's degrees in Social Psychology and Sociology from the [University of California, San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/) (La Jolla, CA). I love both cities for their beautiful weather and the way locals embrace life. If you're planning to visit or move to either city or the surrounding areas, I'd be happy to share recommendations on restaurants, attractions, and transportation.
