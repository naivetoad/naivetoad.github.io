Weiwu Yan

I’m a second-year master’s student in the Computational Social Science program at the University of Chicago (Chicago, IL). My current research focuses on investigating representations of gender in role-playing video game dialogues using natural language processing techniques.

I attended high school at North Broward Preparatory School (Coconut Creek, FL), and earned double bachelor’s degrees in Social Psychology and Sociology from the University of California, San Diego (La Jolla, CA). I love both cities for their beautiful weather and the way locals embrace life. If you’re planning to visit or move to either city or the surrounding areas, I’d be happy to share recommendations on restaurants, attractions, and transportation.

I love traveling internationally and going on road trips, capturing beautiful people, scenery, and architecture through photography.

I’m a big sports fan and enjoy watching soccer, basketball, football, and Formula One.

I love taking on challenges in action role-playing games and teaming up with friends in first-person shooter games.

I enjoy reading comics and watching cartoons and movies that blend comedy and fiction.
